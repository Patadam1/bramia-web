# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS in a single page, hosted on GitHub Pages (public repo) with the custom domain bramia.es, matching how Bramia's client sites are built and deployed.

## Users

Owners of small local businesses in Spain (barbershops, trades such as electricians and air-conditioning installers, restaurants). They usually find Bramia from a reel, a recommendation or a shared link on their phone. Their job: decide whether Bramia is trustworthy enough to talk to about a website or about automating repetitive work.

## Product Purpose

Bramia is Adam's studio for web design and automation for small businesses. The site exists because a studio that sells websites needs a convincing one of its own. Success means a business owner reaches the end of the page and starts a conversation.

## Positioning

Bramia builds custom websites, not templates, and automates the repetitive work around them (forms, notifications, bookings, replies), so the owner gets both visibility and time back. The real client sites already live (bezeta.es, hartielectrocool.es) are the proof.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: custom web design and build, domain and hosting setup, sharing previews (OG images), local SEO basics and Google Business, automations, and brand videos and motion ads (the Bramia ad was made in-house).
- No prices on the site. Quotes are made to measure.
- Contact channels are undecided: a Hostinger email and a WhatsApp business number are coming. Until they exist, the contact buttons stay hidden behind a single config object, and no placeholder number or address is published.
- Domain: bramia.es (DNS not yet pointed).
- Language: Spanish (Spain).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Bramia. Assets: the metallic B mark (`assets/bramia-logo.png`) and the metallic wordmark (`assets/bramia-nombre.png`).
- Palette committed by the user: black + silver, as in the ad (carbon #1C1C1C, graphite #393939, off-white #F5F4F0, stone #D8D6D1, silver #C0C0C0). No orange.
- Fonts already in use: Archivo (heavy, variable width) and Manrope.
- The user loves the ad's typographic posters: heavy Archivo lettering with rows of outlined text sliding behind, and ✦ sparkles.
- Logo ritual: the B appears first and the rest of the name grows out of it.
- Voice: direct, warm and close, in the informal "tú". Tagline from the ad: "Todo negocio merece destacar."

## Evidence on Hand

- Live client work: BEZETA Barberstudio (https://bezeta.es) and Hartielectrocool (https://hartielectrocool.es).
- A third site, Maxó (https://patadam1.github.io/maxo-web/), was made for a prospect; it is not confirmed as a client, so don't show it as one.
- The 42s Bramia ad video.
- No testimonials, client counts or metrics exist. Never invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work instead of describing it: real sites, real details.
2. Every claim must be true today. A missing fact is left out, never invented.
3. Speak like a person from the neighbourhood, not like an agency.
4. One clear next step: talk about your business.
