# Surface: Bramia home (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: small-business owners in Spain, mostly on a phone. Action: start a conversation ("Hablemos de tu negocio"); channels arrive later via the CONTACTO config. Proof: the live sites bezeta.es and hartielectrocool.es (real screenshots). Constraints: no prices, no invented metrics or testimonials, demo notifications and automations are labelled as examples.

Decision: the brief pins the world (black + silver of the ad, typographic posters with outline rows, B-then-wordmark ritual). The roll assigned grounded candidate 4, "the owner's phone lighting up", which is fused into that pinned world. Ran unattended, so this assignment was taken without a decision page. Challengers (dev console, riso zine, ebru, seven-segment, silk canopy, tensegrity) were all declined.
- Raise from seven-segment: absence is drawn too. Outline "ghost" lettering stays visible behind the lit headline.
- Raise from dev console: hairline seams instead of shadows for panels.

## Direction contract
THESIS: the page is what happens to a business once Bramia makes it visible: the phone starts lighting up. It refuses the agency template (split hero, three service cards, logo strip, testimonials).
OWN-WORLD: carbon #1C1C1C ground, graphite seams, off-white Archivo 900 posters with silver full stops, rows of outlined lettering sliding behind, ✦ sparkles, a silver thread line that wipes between sections, and the metallic B mark.
STORY: you don't show up → this is what we build (real sites) → we automate the boring part → talk to us. The visitor believes Bramia makes real, custom things and ends at the contact block.
FIRST VIEWPORT: the giant poster "TODO NEGOCIO MERECE DESTACAR." over sliding outline rows. On the right on desktop (below on mobile, cropped), a phone lock screen where example notifications arrive one by one. The primary button "Hablemos de tu negocio" sits under the headline, and a small nav carries the B mark.
